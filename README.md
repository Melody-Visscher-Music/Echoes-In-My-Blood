# Echoes in My Blood — Story & Game Bible
*Living document — work in progress*

---

## Core Theme

> **Friends and family aren't made. They're found.**

This theme runs through every layer of the game. Xevia tried to engineer connection by creating Meeko as a planned companion — and ended up alone, eventually imprisoned. Anne and Meeko found each other entirely by accident and became family. The contrast between those two outcomes is the emotional spine of the entire story.

## The World

The game is set in a city that begins to experience a strange phenomenon — **echo rifts**. When a rift appears, the surrounding area distorts and transforms into a warped musical realm: neon-soaked, rhythmic, and dangerous. The rifts are not natural. They are the result of a malfunctioning device built by a figure known as Xevia.

Xevia's original plan was to create a single controlled rift — one specific opening that would birth a being designed to be their companion. The device malfunctioned catastrophically. Instead of one rift, over **40 echo rifts** tore open across the city, each one reshaping a section of the urban landscape into a distorted musical level. The being born from the first rift — **Meeko** — developed free will that Xevia never intended or planned for.

The game takes place entirely within the span of a **4 -5 months**. Everything — Meeko's birth, his friendship with Anne, the discovery of his origin, the mid-story fight, the final confrontation — happens within that compressed window. Meeko never reaches a full year of age during the events of the game.

---

## Game Mechanics (Story-Integrated)

**Echo Lives:** The player has three lives per level, called **echoes**. When all lives are lost, the level reshapes itself for the next attempt — gates change, patterns shift. This isn't just a gameplay mechanic. It reflects the nature of the rift world itself: an echo is never an exact copy of what came before. It comes back changed. Meeko carries the memory of every failed run in him, which is why by endgame he reads every pattern before it happens. He is made of accumulated echoes.

**Final Boss Unlock:** The Xevia boss fight only unlocks once the player has completed every single level with **80% accuracy or higher**. The player has to earn being Meeko at full power — mirroring the secret training Meeko does in the story before the final confrontation.

**BPM Arc:**
- Level 1 — 130 BPM (bouncy house / light hardstyle)
- Mid game — melodic, mid-tempo - 160-180 BPM (hardstyle)
- Late game (Meeko track) — 185-220 BPM (rawstyle/hard techno)
- Final boss (Xevia fight) — 230 BPM (Speedcore)

## Characters

### Meeko
**Role:** Protagonist
**Species:** Anthropomorphic fox
**Age at game start:** Unknown

Meeko is born at the very start of the game from the first echo rift — the one Xevia deliberately created. He does not know this. He does not know what he is, where he came from, or why the rifts feel more like home than anywhere else. He just knows that moving through them feels completely natural, like something he was built for. Because he was.

He is hyperactive, warm, and almost completely without suspicion. His joy is not naivety — it is newness. He has never experienced anything that gave him reason to be guarded, so he isn't. By the time the story forces him to become something more serious, it is genuinely jarring, because the player has spent enough time with the happy version of him to feel the weight of that shift.

Meeko is musically attuned in a way no one else in the city is. He doesn't just hear sound — he reads it. Every gate, every corner, every pattern in a rift communicates itself to him through rhythm and tone. This is not a learned skill. It is his fundamental nature, a direct consequence of being born from a musical rift.

**Visual:** Dark/black fur, white chest patch, heterochromia (one blue eye, one purple), red leather jacket, belt. Blonde hair, ear piercing, lip piercing.

**Name origin:** Chosen collaboratively by Meeko and Anne in the early game, during the first weeks of clearing rifts together. Xevia never named him. The name belongs entirely to the relationship that formed without Xevia's involvement.

---

### Anne
**Role:** Ally, scientist, found family
**Species:** Female Cat (grey fur, white muzzle)
**Age at game start:** 18

Anne is a scientist who was already investigating the echo rifts independently before Meeko was born. She detected unusual energy readings from one of the early rifts and went out into the field to study it herself — suggesting she is not the type to wait for institutional approval before acting.

She witnesses Meeko close a rift for the first time. It is the first time she has ever seen one close. She goes after him, expecting to find something strange, and instead finds a hyperactive, grinning kid who seems completely at home in a reality-distorting musical dimension. She can immediately see that he is just a boy — a very unusual boy, but still just a boy.

From that first meeting, the dynamic is set: Anne carries quiet awareness that something about Meeko is extraordinary and possibly complicated, while Meeko simply thinks he found someone interesting to clear rifts with. She never makes him feel like a subject. She shows up, again and again, and that consistency is what builds the relationship into something real.

When the echo shards are discovered, Anne becomes more protective, not more distant. The information doesn't change who Meeko is to her. It just tells her that the threat connected to him is bigger and older than she understood.

**Visual:** Purple and pink hair, red eyes, lab coat worn over alt/goth fashion, striped thigh highs, collar, chain accessories. ID badge reading "ANNE." Purple glowing flask with circuit patterns.

**First meeting:** After Meeko closes the third rift, early game.

## Anne's Minigame Segments

Every 3-5 levels, after Meeko collects data from closed rifts, the game cuts
to Anne's lab for a puzzle segment. The player takes control of Anne and works
at her computer, matching rift signature patterns to unlock research. Each
completed puzzle reveals another piece of lore — details about the rifts,
Meeko's origin, or the Xevia mystery.

Puzzle complexity scales with narrative progress. Early segments are simple
patterns with little data to work from. Later segments become more complex as
the picture gets clearer and the truth gets heavier.

**Mechanical contrast:** Where Meeko reacts to rhythm in real time at high
speed, Anne sits down and thinks. Pattern recognition rather than reflexes.
Slow and methodical rather than instinctive. Two completely different
relationships with the same phenomenon.

**Music:** Harp and piano ambient base as Anne's consistent sonic identity —
precise but warm, analytical but emotional. Distorted fragments of the rift
level Meeko just played layer over that base, because Anne is literally
dissecting the same music he just ran through. Distortion decreases as her
research progresses and the patterns become more understood.

**Visual direction:** Purple tinted screens, circuit patterns consistent with
her character design, lab environment in the background.


### Xevia
**Role:** Antagonist (main game) / Tragic figure (DLC)
**Species:** Anthropomorphic fox (white fur)
**Age at game start:** 20

The only public record Anne can find of Xevia is a missing child report — filed years before either Anne or Meeko existed. Whatever happened to that child during the missing years is unknown. What returned was someone who had been planning the rift device for a very long time, who had erased almost every trace of their own existence, and who was patient enough to wait decades to execute that plan.

Xevia's original intention was not destruction or control. They wanted to create **one** companion — a single being born from a rift who would be with them. The device malfunctioned. Instead of one rift, forty tore open across the city, and the one being who emerged developed a complete independent identity that had nothing to do with Xevia's plan.

Xevia has been watching both Meeko and Anne from a distance for most of the game. They know Meeko's movement patterns well enough to predict which rift he will tackle next. They are never surprised. They are almost always slightly amused — but underneath that is something more complicated.

In the mid-story fight, Xevia attempts to capture or kill Meeko. Meeko barely escapes. By the final boss fight, Meeko defeats them decisively. Xevia is arrested and imprisoned. All rifts close.

**Visual:** White fur, red and black multi-tone hair with a yellow streak, purple eyes, dark crop top, off-shoulder sleeves with chain details, red belt, chain-adorned trousers. Choker, necklace. Expression: controlled, smirking, always seems to know more than they say.

**Age & Timeline:**
Xevia was six years old when they disappeared. Over the following 18 years
they researched and built the rift device entirely alone. At age 20 they
powered the machine — which immediately malfunctioned, creating 40 rifts
instead of one, and producing a being with complete free will they never
planned for.

Xevia's entire adolescence was spent in isolation working toward a single
goal: to not be alone. By the time they achieved it, it failed.

*Where Xevia disappeared to and what those 18 years contained is the central
mystery of the DLC — not yet developed.*

## Story Beats

### Early Game

**Level 1 — Birth**
Meeko comes into existence inside the first rift. His opening experience is pure sensory wonder — light, sound, movement, the rift world reshaping around him. He doesn't question it. It feels completely right. He closes the rift because it simply feels like what he is supposed to do and its fun for him. from here on out he starts to search for other rifts to clear.

**After Level 3 — First Meeting**
Anne has been tracking unusual energy readings and arrives at a rift to investigate. Meeko shoots past her in a blur of speed and rhythm and the rift closes — the first time she has ever witnessed a closure. She goes after him. She finds a happy, hyperactive kid who has no idea why she looks so stunned. Anne feels responsible for such a young kid, They begin clearing rifts together.

**The Name**
During the early weeks of clearing rifts together, Meeko and Anne land on a name for him. It emerges naturally from time spent together rather than as a formal decision. Xevia had no part in naming him. The name Meeko belongs entirely to this relationship.

Meeko and Anne both bond and start being a found family, their bond grows close and anne and meeko actually care about eachother (platonic).
in this time the player gets the changes to buy upgrades and small qol stuff that will help in the rest of the journey.

### Mid Game

**Echo Shards**
Anne's ongoing research into Meeko's unusual nature eventually leads to a blood analysis. She finds echo shards — fragments of rift material — embedded in his bloodstream. This is the moment the title of the game stops being a metaphor. The shards are evidence not just that Meeko was born from a rift, but that the rift was specifically constructed. He was not an accident of the phenomenon. He was the intended result of it.

The track *Echoes in My Blood* plays during this section of the game. The player has been hearing it as a cool lyric and meeko just sang it cuz of the echo rifts. Then the scientist Anne finds literal echo shards in his blood. The song title becomes a diagnosis.

Anne tells Meeko. He takes it badly.

**Meeko Throws Himself Into Work**
Rather than processing the discovery, Meeko goes back into the rifts and clears them relentlessly. He doesn't spiral or seek comfort. He just moves, because moving is all he knows how to do when something is wrong. This is consistent with who he is — same instinct as finishing the rift before going to Anne after the first Xevia encounter.

**First Xevia Encounter**
Meeko runs through a rift level and Xevia is simply there — calm, already waiting, watching him with an expression that isn't quite how a stranger looks at someone they've just met. Xevia says only that they have been watching Meeko, and that they have been waiting for this meeting. Nothing threatening. Nothing that Meeko can clearly identify as wrong. Just deeply, quietly unsettling.

Meeko closes the rift. Then he goes to Anne.

**Anne Notices**
She can read his baseline well enough by this point to know immediately that something is off. He's slightly too still. For a character whose entire identity is constant forward motion, stillness is the alarm signal. When Meeko describes what happened, he does it almost factually at first — and only partway through realises how strange the encounter actually was.

**Anne Researches Xevia**
She searches for the name and finds almost nothing. One record: a missing child, filed years before either Anne or Meeko was born. Xevia is far older than they appear. Whatever happened during those missing years left no trace. Anne tells Meeko. There isn't much to tell.

---

### Late Game / Approaching the Finale

**The Mid-Story Fight**
Eventually Meeko encounters Xevia again — this time not a conversation. Xevia attempts to capture or kill him. The fight is brutal. Meeko escapes, but barely — multiple cuts, bruises, two or three broken bones. He comes back to Anne visibly hurt and genuinely scared, which is the first time she has ever seen him scared.

Anne patches him up. She holds him. He is less than a year old. The game started at his birth.

**Secret Training**
Without telling Anne, Meeko goes back into the rifts and begins systematically seeking out the hardest challenges he can find. He trains relentlessly. Anne notices he seems different — more focused, slightly less chaotic — but doesn't know why. He is carrying the preparation alone, the same way Anne carried worry about him alone for most of the game. The roles have quietly reversed.

**The Final Boss — Xevia (210 BPM)**
Meeko wins. Easily. The secret training means he walks into the confrontation genuinely ready. Xevia is not prepared for what Meeko has become in the months since their last fight.

At some point during or after the confrontation, the truth comes out — that Xevia created the rift Meeko was born from. That this was intentional. That Meeko was supposed to be a companion, not a nemesis.

Xevia is arrested. All 40 rifts close simultaneously.

## DLC — Xevia's Story
*(Planned — not yet developed)*

The DLC takes the player through Xevia's life before the events of the main game — specifically the missing years that left no record. The player experiences what turned a disappeared child into someone who would spend decades building a device to create a companion.

The DLC ends with the player watching Xevia activate the device — and seeing it malfunction. Watching Meeko emerge and immediately become something that doesn't need them. Watching everything they waited decades for simply not go the way they planned.

**The final revelation:** Xevia is not a villain. They are a hurt child who wanted a friend and accidentally created their greatest nemesis instead. The main game recontextualises completely once this is understood.

This is told as DLC specifically so it doesn't soften Xevia during the main game. The mystery needs to stay intact for the story to work. The DLC is optional context — for players who want to understand, after the fact, what they were actually fighting.
